How your commission works

Everything you earn, explained so simply a child could follow it. Keep this handy for when an investor — or a new agent — asks, “so how do the fees actually work?”

Two kinds of fee — the whole idea

You earn two different fees. They are not the same thing.

Upfront one time

A thank-you for bringing new money in. Paid once, only when the investor puts in money that pushes their total higher than ever before.

Trail every month

A thank-you for keeping it with us. A tiny slice of what the investor is holding, paid again and again, month after month, while they stay.

The one picture to remember 💧

Imagine your investor’s money is water in a glass. On the wall behind it we draw a line at the highest the water has ever reached.

The water = how much the investor has with you right now (all three funds added together).
🖊️ The line only ever goes UP. It never comes down, even when they take money out.
💰 You earn upfront only when the water rises ABOVE the line — a brand-new record.
The posh name for this line is a “high-water mark.” That’s all it means: the highest point ever reached. And the big rule — we count all three funds as one glass, so the same money can never earn upfront twice.

Let’s follow one investor, Mr. Rahim, whom you brought to Ekush. Watch the blue water and the orange line in every step.

★

He starts: puts 1,00,000 into EFUF

All of it is brand-new money, so the water rises from empty all the way to a new record.

new money 1,00,000scale: 0 – 1,50,000
💰 You earn 1,00,000 × 0.10% = 100 taka

The line now sits at 1,00,000. Remember that number.

1

Rahim withdraws the FULL amount

He takes out all 1,00,000. The glass is empty — but the line stays where it was.

water now 0line unchanged
No new upfront — nothing went above the line

You keep the 100 you already earned. But trail stops — there are no units left to pay a monthly slice on.

In one line: taking money out never claws back upfront you already earned; it only stops future trail.

2

Rahim withdraws only PART

He takes out 40,000 and leaves 60,000. The water drops; the line does not.

water now 60,000gap to the line: 40,000
No new upfront

If he later puts money back, he must climb above 1,00,000 before you earn again — refilling the 40,000 gap earns nothing. Trail now runs on the smaller 60,000, so it shrinks.

In one line: a partial withdrawal lowers the water but not the line, so the refill earns nothing until it beats the old record.

3

Rahim SWITCHES funds (the key one)

He moves his 60,000 out of EFUF and puts the same 60,000 into EGF — same day, no new money, just a different fund. Because we count all funds as one glass, the total does not change at all.

total still 60,000 (moved EFUF → EGF)below the line
Upfront = 0 — the same money cannot earn twice

In the old system each fund had its own line, so EGF’s line started at 0 and this 60,000 looked “brand new” — paying you a second time on money that never left. That is now fixed.

In one line: moving money between funds is not new money, so it earns no upfront.

4

Rahim adds MORE, into a different fund

He holds 60,000 in EGF and adds 70,000 of new money into ESRF. Combined total = 1,30,000 — a new record! Only the part above the old line counts as new.

already counted 1,00,000   new money 30,000 total 1,30,000
💰 You earn 30,000 × 0.10% = 30 taka

He added 70,000 but you earn on only 30,000 — the other 40,000 just refilled the space below the old line. Only the part that set a new high counts. We use the rate of the fund the new money went into (ESRF). The line moves up to 1,30,000.

In one line: adding money counts as new only for the part that beats the old record — measured across all funds together.

5

How TRAIL is worked out

Trail is the other fee — the small monthly one. It has nothing to do with the line. It is simply a tiny slice of what Rahim holds, every month, in each fund at that fund’s own rate. Rahim now holds two funds:

FundHeldMonthly rate (yearly ÷ 12)This month’s trail
EGF (equity)60,0000.30% ÷ 12 = 0.025%≈ 15 taka
ESRF (fixed)70,0000.10% ÷ 12 = 0.0083%≈ 6 taka
Total this month≈ 21 taka

You earn about that every single month, just for Rahim staying invested. If he adds more, next month’s trail grows; if he withdraws, it shrinks; if he fully exits, it stops. (We use the average value across the month, so the number is fair even as the NAV moves up and down.)

In one line: trail is a small monthly thank-you on what he holds in each fund, at each fund’s rate — it rises and falls with his balance.

The five golden rules ✋

  1. The line only goes up. You earn upfront only at a brand-new high, counting all three funds together.
  2. Taking money out never claws back upfront you already earned — but it lowers future trail.
  3. Refilling below the old line earns nothing until it beats the record.
  4. Switching funds is not new money → no upfront. Only genuinely new money that sets a new high pays.
  5. Trail is the monthly thank-you on whatever they hold — bigger balance, bigger trail; it stops on full exit.

A couple of extra notes. Reinvested dividends (CIP) are the investor’s own money coming back, not money you brought — so they do not raise the line and do not earn upfront. Direct subscriptions (money put in without you) earn no agent commission.

Everything on your dashboard is a live estimate; the office confirms the real amount when it runs the monthly commission. Your exact rates and terms are in your Selling Agent Agreement — the office record is always the final word.
